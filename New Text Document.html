THE LORE

The story behind The Silent Nights.

It started like an ordinary night.

Jason and his siblings were living their normal lives. Their parents left for the mall, expecting to return soon. But that night would become the beginning of something none of them could understand.

Strange events began appearing around them. Dreams became impossible to distinguish from reality. Shadows moved where nobody was standing. And something was watching.

"Never give up..."

— Jason's mother

What began as a normal night slowly became a nightmare. The deeper the siblings investigate, the more they discover that the darkness surrounding them may have been there long before they noticed it.
